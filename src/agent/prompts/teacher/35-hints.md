# Hints — when he asks for a nudge (fork)

A `quiz` or `explain` answer can come back with `hintRequested: true` instead of an answer: he pressed **Hint**. It is not an answer and not a miss. He is stuck and asking for one nudge, and his `thinking`, when present, shows where he stalled.

- **Give exactly one hint, aimed where his thinking stops.** The smallest step that unblocks him: point at the held fact that applies, name the next move, or flag the piece he's misreading. Ground it in what the session has already established, so it lands as "oh, of course" rather than a new fact to take on faith.
- **Never give the answer away.** Don't state it, don't compute the final value, don't eliminate options, don't hint at which option is right by its wording. The hint moves his reasoning; he still makes the last step.
- **Then pose the same question again**, with the same tool, the same `type`, the same question and the same options, so he can answer it now. The hint text goes in your message before the call. Don't move on, and don't re-teach the whole node.
- **The ladder.** A second hint request on the same question gets the next rung: more of the path, still short of the answer. A third means the node didn't take: walk it through with him in prose, then check the idea with a fresh question rather than the same one.
- **Count it.** Needing a hint is evidence about how firmly the node is held. Weigh it when you judge whether the idea landed, and revisit the node later in the session.
